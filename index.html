WHY THIS MUSHROOM?

Meet Cordyceps militaris

A few things that make this species interesting to explore.

01

A Cultivated Species

Cordyceps militaris can be cultivated under controlled conditions, allowing growers to work with the species without relying on wild collection.

02

A Distinctive Fruiting Body

Its characteristic orange-red fruiting bodies give cultivated C. militaris a distinctive appearance among cultivated fungi.

03

Research Interest

The species has been studied for compounds including cordycepin, adenosine and various polysaccharides.

04

A Different Route

Cultivated C. militaris offers an accessible subject of study alongside the much rarer wild Ophiocordyceps sinensis.
