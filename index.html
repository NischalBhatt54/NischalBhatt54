OUR PHILOSOPHY

What is SHILA?

SHILA was built around a simple idea: quality before quantity.

Rather than pursuing larger and larger production volumes, we deliberately cultivate Cordyceps militaris in limited batches. This allows us to devote greater attention to the cultivation environment and to each stage of the growing process.

Cordyceps militaris is a specialized, resource-intensive cultivated product. For SHILA, careful cultivation is not an afterthought—it is the foundation of the brand.

OUR STORY

Why SHILA exists

Growing up in Dehradun, with roots in Pauri Garhwal, the Himalayas and the traditional herbs associated with the region were always familiar.

Curiosity about Himalayan Cordyceps led us to Cordyceps militaris, which we initially began cultivating as a hobby for personal consumption.

Encouraging results across multiple growing cycles changed the idea from a personal experiment into something we wanted to develop further: a small, quality-focused business built around careful cultivation.

SHILA grew from the desire to share that same care and attention to quality with others.
