Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information we may collect when you interact with the SHILA Foods website and how that information may be used.

Last updated: August 2026
01

Introduction

Welcome to the website of SHILA Foods ("SHILA", "we", "us", or "our").

SHILA Foods is a proprietorship-based business located in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, India, focused on the cultivation and development of Cordyceps militaris and related functional food products.

We respect the privacy of visitors to our website and aim to handle personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit or interact with our website, why we may use that information, and the choices available to you.

02

About this website

At present, the SHILA Foods website is primarily an informational and educational website.

It provides information about SHILA Foods, cultivated Cordyceps militaris, our cultivation approach, and related educational material.

At the time this Privacy Policy was published, the website does not require visitors to create user accounts and does not provide an online checkout or direct e-commerce facility.

If online purchasing, user accounts, newsletters, subscriptions, or other services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect those changes.

03

Information we may collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with our website.

Information you provide voluntarily

If you contact us by email, phone, WhatsApp, or through a contact form if one is available, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Your message or enquiry
  • Other information you choose to provide in your communication

You are not required to provide information beyond what is reasonably necessary for us to respond to your enquiry.

Automatically collected information

Like many websites, our website hosting infrastructure may automatically process certain technical information when a page is requested.

Depending on the hosting and technical services being used, this may include information such as IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, requested pages, and basic access timestamps.

This information is generally used for purposes such as website operation, security, debugging, and understanding technical problems.

04

How we use information

Information provided to us may be used for legitimate business and communication purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and messages
  • Communicating with potential customers, existing customers, suppliers, or other individuals who contact us
  • Providing information requested by you
  • Improving the website and its content
  • Identifying and addressing technical or security issues
  • Maintaining the functioning and security of our website
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

We do not intend to use personal information for purposes unrelated to the reason for which it was provided, except where permitted or required by applicable law.

05

Email and direct communications

If you contact SHILA Foods directly, we may retain your communication and contact details for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain appropriate business records, or comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your email address or contact information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

If you contact us regarding a potential purchase, product enquiry, collaboration, wholesale enquiry, or other business matter, the information you provide may be used to communicate with you regarding that matter.

06

WhatsApp and third-party communication

If you choose to contact SHILA Foods through WhatsApp, your communication will also be subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

WhatsApp is an external communication service and is not operated or controlled by SHILA Foods.

We recommend that you avoid sending sensitive personal information through WhatsApp or ordinary email unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

07

Cookies and similar technologies

The SHILA Foods website may use cookies or similar technologies depending on the services and functionality implemented on the website.

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can serve functions such as remembering preferences, supporting website functionality, or providing technical information about website usage.

If analytics, advertising, embedded content, or other third-party services are introduced, those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies.

You can generally control or delete cookies through the settings of your web browser.

08

Third-party services and links

Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties.

These may include social media platforms, communication services, scientific publications, research papers, external references, or other websites that we believe may be useful to visitors.

Once you leave the SHILA Foods website, the privacy practices of the third-party website will apply. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party websites before providing them with personal information.

09

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information where reasonably necessary with service providers or other parties who help us operate our website or conduct our business, where such sharing is appropriate and permitted by applicable law.

We may also disclose information where we believe that disclosure is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, respond to a lawful request, protect our rights, or address security or fraud concerns.

10

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information that is provided to us from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Therefore, while we make reasonable efforts to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to us through the internet.

11

Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain appropriate business records, to resolve disputes, or to comply with applicable legal obligations.

The period for which information is retained may therefore vary depending on the nature of the information and the reason it was collected.

12

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding personal information that we hold about you.

These may include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or asking questions about how your information is being used.

If you would like to make a privacy-related request, you can contact us using the details provided below.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on certain requests in order to protect your information from unauthorized access.

13

Children's privacy

Our website is intended to provide general information about SHILA Foods and Cordyceps militaris.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through the website.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us so that we can consider appropriate action.

14

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our website, business practices, technology, or applicable legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically if they wish to remain informed about our privacy practices.
