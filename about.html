OUR APPROACH

What is SHILA?

SHILA Foods is a Dehradun-based functional foods brand dedicated to the careful cultivation of Cordyceps militaris.

We created SHILA around a simple belief: quality should come before quantity. Rather than pursuing large-scale production, we deliberately cultivate in limited batches so that every growing cycle receives greater attention and consistency.

For us, Cordyceps militaris is not simply another mushroom. It is a specialized cultivated organism that deserves patience, precision, and respect throughout its entire lifecycle.

Quality is not something we inspect at the end. It is something we cultivate from the beginning.

WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

Curiosity became cultivation.

Every brand begins with a story. Ours began with curiosity.

Growing up in Dehradun, with roots in the Himalayan region of Pauri Garhwal, traditional Himalayan herbs and natural botanicals were always familiar.

Among them was Ophiocordyceps sinensis — the legendary caterpillar fungus found in the high Himalayas.

Learning about its rarity and remarkable biology eventually led us to another species within the same broader group: Cordyceps militaris.

What started as reading and research soon became a personal experiment.

THE BEGINNING OF SHILA

From hobby to SHILA.

The original goal was never to build a business.

We simply wanted to cultivate Cordyceps militaris for personal consumption and to understand whether a mushroom often described as challenging to grow could be produced successfully in a small indoor setup.

The first cultivation cycles exceeded our expectations. Rather than stopping there, we continued refining the process — improving environmental control, substrate preparation, and cultivation techniques with every batch.

Perhaps the most valuable outcome wasn't the harvest itself. It was the confidence that came from knowing exactly how the mushrooms had been grown.

For a specialized, high-value product that is often susceptible to inconsistent quality and adulteration, that level of control mattered.

That confidence eventually became SHILA.

WHY CORDYCEPS?

Why Cordyceps militaris?

A fascinating organism at the intersection of traditional knowledge and modern cultivation.

Cordyceps militaris has gained increasing attention within the world of functional foods because of its unique biology and the naturally occurring compounds it contains.

These include compounds such as cordycepin, adenosine, and various polysaccharides, which have been the subject of scientific research.

While its wild relative, Ophiocordyceps sinensis, remains exceptionally rare and expensive, cultivated Cordyceps militaris can be produced under controlled conditions without relying on harvesting from fragile alpine ecosystems.

Our focus is not on making extraordinary health claims. We believe people deserve clear, research-based information and a carefully cultivated product whose origin they can trust.
